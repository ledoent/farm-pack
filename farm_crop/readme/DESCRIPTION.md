Catalog of crops grown on the farm. Seeds with 18 common US small-farm crops at
install (tomato, lettuce, kale, sweet corn, etc.) and lets farmers add their own.

Used by `farm_field` (current crop on a field), `farm_planting` (what's being
planted), and `farm_harvest` (what's being harvested) — but those modules don't
ship together; this catalog is the single source of crop identity.
